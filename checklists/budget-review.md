# Budget Review Checklist

- [ ] reporting period identified
- [ ] budget version/source identified
- [ ] actual source identified
- [ ] revenue compared to budget
- [ ] expense compared to budget
- [ ] material variances identified
- [ ] restricted/grant amounts separated where applicable
- [ ] class/project coding exceptions listed
- [ ] unusual activity listed
- [ ] unresolved items separated from verified amounts
- [ ] management/board decisions identified
