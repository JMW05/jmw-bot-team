# byJMW

## Type
Client-facing website/services brand and umbrella for JMW projects.

## Goal
The site itself must demonstrate strong custom web capability: premium design, purposeful motion, clear service requests/packages, affordability without looking cheap, and evidence that JMW can handle varied website/workflow projects.

## Brand direction
byJMW is the preferred umbrella domain/brand. The visual identity should feel custom and capable, not template-based. Motion is encouraged when it supports the story rather than distracting from it.

## Critical experience
- clear positioning
- service/package explanation
- request-services flow
- portfolio/case-study proof
- premium responsive presentation
- credible process/QA language
- fast path to contact/request work

## Quality bar
This is a showcase site. Hold it to the strictest Design Director standard because the site is evidence of the service being sold.

## Critical QA
portfolio links, forms, package/pricing copy, motion, mobile, performance, accessibility, staging vs production, analytics/integrations if present.
