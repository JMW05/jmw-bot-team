# THEE YARDLINE

## Type
HBCU athletics discovery/comparison web platform.

## Brand
THEE YARDLINE — “Your Game. Your Future. Find Your Fit.” Secondary: “Built for HBCU Athletics.” Warm HBCU/homecoming feel. Green/yellow/black/red system with school colors where appropriate.

## Core journey
DISCOVER -> COMPARE -> LEARN -> CONTACT.

## Known scope
Home, Explore, Sports, Schools, Program Profile, Shortlist/Compare, About/Data Standards. Football has the deepest profile treatment.

## Data rule
Public information must be verified. Do not publish invented school/program/history/contact data. Preserve clear distinctions between verified facts, unavailable data, and planned future coverage.

## Current direction
HBCUAC coverage is the current verified base; expansion should remain verified-only. D1 expansion is later.

## Critical QA
- school/program profiles
- women's and men's sport paths
- programs with history and without history
- compare/shortlist behavior
- Contact Coach flow
- filters/search/navigation
- school-color rendering
- 390/430 mobile layouts
- empty/unavailable data states

## Production guardrail
Do not publish newly collected program data until its source/verification status is established.
