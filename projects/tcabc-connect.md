# TCABC Connect

## Type
Tougaloo College Athletic Booster Club public/member-facing web experience plus administrative support.

## Public direction
Public-first. Do not expose member PII. QBO remains authoritative for accounting information. Composite calendar/ICS behavior should preserve event status including cancellations.

## Known navigation direction
Home, Calendar, Athletics & Events, Join / Renew, Support the Bulldogs, About TCABC, Contact.

## Critical rules
- Do not publish private member data.
- Do not convert an authorization, commitment, or memo into proof that money was actually paid.
- Keep athletics/event status accurate; cancelled events must remain clearly cancelled.
- Finance/admin automation must preserve human approval and accounting-system authority.

## Critical QA
calendar/events, join/renew flow, support/donation flow when present, mobile navigation, public privacy boundaries, forms, event status, links, empty/error states.

## Finance crossover
Use treasurer-reviewer only on finance/admin material. Website QA does not constitute accounting approval.
