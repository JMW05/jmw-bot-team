# TCRDF

## Type
501(c)(3) finance/treasurer operations and internal workflow project.

## Accounting authority
QBO is authoritative. Melio supports AP/payment operations. Finance automation must remain review-first unless a future explicitly approved workflow changes that boundary.

## Known finance concerns
- bank reconciliation and cash position
- budget vs actual
- grant/project coding
- W-9 collection
- 1099 tracking
- payment/support packages
- IRS/name-change and entity records
- audit trail and approval evidence

## Known projects/programs
DARPA, GTRI, SCALE, DOE, REDI, plus ERISSC startup activity. Agents must not assume every transaction belongs to one of these; use actual source data.

## AI may
review, reconcile, flag exceptions, draft reports, prepare proposed entries, prepare import-ready data for human review.

## AI may not
release/schedule payment, post journal entries, alter QBO, change vendor banking, file tax/compliance documents, or treat review status as approval.

## Critical review output
Separate verified support, apparent support, missing support, coding questions, compliance flags, management questions, and human decisions required.
